## Core positioning

Playstore Wizard turns a confusing Google Play release into a guided 12-step launch plan. Developers can complete the first six steps free, save their progress on their device, and unlock the remaining readiness steps with Pro. It prepares the information and assets needed for submission; the developer submits through their own Google Play Console.

Do not claim automatic Google Play publishing, guaranteed approval, guaranteed revenue, unlimited apps, team workspaces, or AdSense earnings.
