# Campaign posting checklist

Before each public post:

- Confirm the visible account and platform.
- Confirm the final copy, video, thumbnail, tracking link, and audience.
- Check the current community's self-promotion rules.
- Do not claim affiliation with Google or guaranteed Play Store approval.
- Do not request Product Hunt upvotes.
- Confirm the post action immediately before publishing.

After each public post:

- Record the public URL and timestamp.
- Verify the tracking link opens Playstore Wizard successfully.
- Verify the video plays with captions and the domain remains readable.
- Reply to genuine questions without copying canned marketing text.
- Record traffic, wizard starts, step-6 completion, checkout starts, and purchases.
